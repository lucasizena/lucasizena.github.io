OpenAI just claimed a solution to the Navier-Stokes existance and smoothness problem! I've been hearing rumours recently about developments on stuff related to Navier-Stokes, like [the recent work of Alpöge and Buckmaster](https://mastodon.social/@tristanbuckmaster/117233413705701198) on some simpler model equations (I think the incompressible porous medium (IPM) equation, two-dimensional Boussinesq equation, and three-dimensional incompressible Euler equations), so many people did actually expect that using similar strategies could work on the Navier-Stokes problem. Apparently it did cost a lot of compute though. There's also a lot of controversy going on
