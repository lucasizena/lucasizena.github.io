Hi! I'm Lucas. Some of my interests include maths, maths olympiads, machine learning, and deep learning. I currently live in Coventry, UK, and I'm a Sixth Form student at The National Mathematics and Science College. 

Most of the times in this (micro) blog I'll be talking about these topics related to my interests, but occasionally I'll discuss other unrelated things like learning or random thoughts that pop up in my head throughout the day. 

I made this blog mostly for myself because I thought that it would be interesting to keep a record of some of the things I do, but it might be that you find something useful here. I will try to post things that may be of use to others, such as walking you through my thought process on how I solve problems in, say, mathematics. 

As such, you can subscribe to this blog using the [Atom feed](/atom.xml).

You can also find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/lucasizena), LinkedIn, or email me at [lucas.izena@gmail.com](lucas.izena@gmail.com) or [lucas.izena@natmatsci.ac.uk](lucas.izena@natmatsci.ac.uk).
