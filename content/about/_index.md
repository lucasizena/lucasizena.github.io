Hi! I'm Lucas. Some of my interests include maths, maths olympiads, deep learning, and programming. I'm currently a sixth form student in the UK. 

Most of the times in this blog I write about topics related to my interests, but occasionally I discuss unrelated things like random thoughts that pop up in my head throughout the day[^yap]. You can check out the different topics of each of my previous posts in the [Archive](https://lucasizena.github.io/archive/).

I made this blog mostly for myself because I thought it'd be interesting to keep a record of some of the things I do (as well as to see how my interests and way of thinking develop as time progresses), and as such a lot of the posts here may be slightly disorganised or unpolished, but they will always be honest reflections of what I genuinely thought at the time. For this reason, please don't take anything that I write here too seriously! Meanwhile, I'll (sometimes) try to post things that may be of use, such as giving instructive walkthroughs through maths problems, writing about how I learn and improve myself, or discussing some interesting topic that I've recently come across. 

You can follow this blog using the [Atom feed](/atom.xml).

You can also find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/lucasizena) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/lucas-izena-a39315386/). You can reach me out at [lucas.izena@gmail.com](mailto:lucas.izena@gmail.com). If you find a bug or typo, or have any suggestions, please open an issue or pull request on [GitHub](https://github.com/lucasizena/lucasizena.github.io) or just email me.
